# Privacy Policy — Petzara

**Last updated (Portuguese original):** 10/16/2026

---

## Legal notice

This Policy describes how the operator of the **Petzara** platform ("**we**", "**our**" or the "**operator**") processes personal data in connection with the software-as-a-service (SaaS) product offered to pet service businesses. **It does not replace legal advice.** The operator's full corporate identification (legal name, CNPJ (Brazilian company registration number) and address) **will be published on this page when available**; until then, you can request information through the contact channel listed in section 14.

This Policy is aligned with **Brazilian Law No. 13,709/2018 (General Data Protection Law — LGPD)**.

---

## 1. Controller and data protection officer (DPO)

**1.1** The **controller** of the personal data processed on behalf of the operator of the Petzara Platform is the **Petzara operator** itself, to the extent that it determines the purposes and means of processing.

**1.2** The **data protection officer (DPO)**, once appointed, may be contacted at the email listed in section 14. Until an appointment is made public, LGPD-related requests may be sent to the **contact channel** listed in section 14.

**1.3** Businesses that use Petzara to process data about **their** clients and pet owners may act as **controllers** or **processors** with respect to those data subjects, as the case may be. These Users are responsible for providing appropriate notices and legal bases to **their** data subjects.

---

## 2. Scope: who the data subjects are under this Policy

**2.1** This Policy mainly applies to the processing of data about:

- **Platform Users** (administrators and team members of businesses);
- on an **ancillary** basis, data about **end customers, pet owners and animals** that the business **records** in Petzara (for example, in appointments or records).

**2.2** The **public availability page** linked to the business's URL **does not require the pet owner to log in**; Petzara **does not collect identified personal data** from visitors **on that screen** beyond what the browser and usual providers process (see section 8). Subsequent contact may take place through **WhatsApp** or another external channel, and the content of that conversation is outside the control of this Policy.

---

## 3. Personal data we may process

Depending on the features used, we may process the categories below.

### 3.1 Account and authentication data (Users)

- Identification: **name**, **business name**, **email address**, **phone number**;
- authentication: **password hash**, **email verification** tokens, **password recovery** tokens, **Google identifier** when social login is used;
- session: **access token** (JWT) and **refresh token** (for example, in an **httpOnly cookie**);
- relationships: **role** (administrator or team member), reference to the **account holder** (owner), **department**, invitations;
- preferences: **theme**, **calendar sort order**, **public URL**, **URL activation**, **simultaneous service limits**, **working hours settings** and similar.

### 3.2 Subscription and payment data (SaaS)

- Subscription identifiers and status with the payment provider used: **Google Play** (purchase token, order number, product, plan and, when the purchase uses a Google Play free period, the offer identifier and the end date of that period) or **Stripe** (customer, subscription); **current period**, **trial period**, billing dates, status (active, in trial, past due, canceled, etc.).

We do **not** process the **full** credit card number on our application servers; payment processing is carried out by **Google Play** or **Stripe**, depending on the provider used for the purchase.

### 3.3 Operational data entered by the business

- **Clients:** name, email, phone number, **address** (street, number, unit, neighborhood, city, state, postal code), notes;
- **Pets:** name, species, breed, size, date of birth, notes, optional link to a client;
- **Appointments:** animal and owner details (name, phone number), services, amounts, date and time, status, staff member responsible;
- **Finances:** entries (description, amounts, categories, dates, payment methods, links to appointments, depending on use);
- **Notifications** generated by the Platform.

### 3.4 Communication and support data

- Messages sent through authenticated **support** channels (name, email, subject, content);
- records of **transactional emails** (account confirmation, invitations, password reset), sent through an email provider.

### 3.5 Technical and security data

- **Error and performance** records through tools such as **Sentry** (may include technical device and browser data, **IP address** in incidents, stack traces);
- server and traffic **logs** at the infrastructure level, where applicable;
- information needed for **fraud prevention** and account **security**.

## 4. Purposes of processing

We process personal data to:

1. **Register, authenticate and manage accounts** of administrators and team members;
2. **Provide and operate** Petzara's features (calendar, records, finances, notifications, public availability page);
3. **Bill and manage subscriptions** (including the trial period, when offered), through Google Play or Stripe;
4. **Communicate** with the User (transactional email, support);
5. **Comply with legal obligations** and respond to legitimate requests from authorities;
6. **Protect rights**, prevent fraud and ensure the **security** of the Platform;
7. **Improve the stability and quality** of the service (error diagnostics with Sentry and related technical data);
8. **Comply with this Policy** and the [Terms of Use](./TERMS_OF_USE.md).

## 5. Legal bases (LGPD)

Depending on the case, we rely on the following legal bases, under Article 7 of the LGPD:

| Purpose (summary) | Typical legal basis |
|---------------------|---------------------|
| Performance of the Platform use agreement | **Performance of a contract** (item III) |
| Account creation and management; billing | **Performance of a contract** and **regular exercise of rights** (item VI), where applicable |
| Compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation | **Legal obligation** (item II) |
| Support, security, fraud prevention | **Legitimate interest** (item IX), with an assessment of the data subject's expectations and a balancing test |
| Essential transactional communications | **Performance of a contract** or **legitimate interest** in informing the data subject about the service |
| Data entered by the business about its clients | Generally, **performance of the contract** between the operator and the business; the business is accountable to its data subjects |

Where **consent** is required (for example, for optional, non-essential communications), it will be requested prominently.

In these cases, we adopt the measures provided for in the LGPD (Articles 33 to 36), such as **standard contractual clauses**, **certifications** or other compatible safeguards, and we inform data subjects of this possibility in this Policy.

## 8. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

**8.1** We use **cookies** that are strictly necessary for the session, including an **httpOnly** cookie for the **refresh token** (security and domain settings depend on the environment, for example `.petzara.app` in production).

**8.2** The **access token** may be stored in the **browser's local storage** (`localStorage` or similar) to keep the User signed in.

**8.3** Use of **"Sign in with Google"** is subject to **Google's** cookie and privacy policies.

**8.4** On the **company website** (petzara.app) we may use third-party **audience measurement** and **advertising** tools — **Google Analytics 4** (Google) and **Meta Pixel** (Meta Platforms) — to understand how the site is used (page views, clicks on "create account", "WhatsApp" and "Google Play") and to measure campaigns. These tools **are only loaded after you consent** in the cookie banner ("Accept"). If you click "Decline", they are not loaded. Your choice is saved in the browser's local storage and can be changed at any time under **"Cookie preferences"** in the site footer. Legal basis: **consent** (Article 7, I, of the LGPD). Data collected by these tools is also processed under Google's and Meta's policies and may be processed outside Brazil (see section 7).

**8.5** The **system** (app.petzara.app) and the **Android app** do not use advertising cookies for marketing profiling.

## 9. Information security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures, such as:

- **HTTPS** communication in production;
- password **hashing**;
- **short-lived** access tokens with controlled renewal;
- rate limiting on sensitive operations (for example, checkout);
- error monitoring and restricted infrastructure access.

No system is completely secure. In the event of a **security incident** that may pose a risk to data subjects, we will notify the authorities and those affected when required by law.

## 10. Data retention

**10.1** We keep data for as long as necessary to **fulfill the purposes** described, in compliance with legal retention periods (tax, civil, etc.).

**10.2** **Accounts that do not subscribe** at the end of the free trial switch to read-only mode, and their data (registration, clients, pets, appointments and finances) **remains stored for as long as the account exists** — there is no automatic deletion for lack of a subscription. The User may, at any time, **export** data (for example, appointments as CSV or XLSX, in Settings; other data upon request through the channel in section 14) or **request deletion** of the account (under **Settings › Delete account** or through the channel in section 14).

**10.3** After an **account is closed**, we may **retain** data for an additional period to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes or regularly exercise rights.

**10.4** Logs and technical security data may be retained for **shorter or longer** periods, according to internal policy and the law.

## 11. Data subject rights (LGPD)

Under Article 18 of the LGPD, data subjects may request:

- **confirmation** that processing exists;
- **access** to the data;
- **correction** of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
- **anonymization, blocking or deletion** of unnecessary or excessive data, or data processed in breach of the LGPD;
- **portability**, where applicable;
- **deletion** of data processed based on consent, subject to legal exceptions;
- **information** about data sharing;
- **withdrawal** of consent, where the basis is consent.

**11.1** Requests may be made through the **contact channel** in section 14. We may request information to **verify identity**.

**11.2** Some requests can be **handled directly by the User** on the Platform itself (editing records, exporting appointments and deleting the account). Data processed by **Stripe** or **Google** may also require a request made directly to those providers.

**11.3** Data subjects may **file a petition with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD)**.
